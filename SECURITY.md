# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately to **security@tavorix.xyz**.
Do not open a public issue or post details publicly before we have had a chance to fix the problem.

Include, where possible:
- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Affected files, contracts, or addresses
- Your contact details if you would like credit

## What to expect

We aim to acknowledge reports promptly and to keep you informed as we investigate and fix the issue.
We ask for reasonable time to fix a problem before any public disclosure.

## Scope

In scope: code in this repository (smart contracts, scanner, backend).
Out of scope: social engineering, phishing of team members, denial-of-service testing against live infrastructure, and third-party services.

## Status

The smart contracts in this repository are **unaudited drafts** and must not be used with real funds.
A formal bug bounty has not been launched yet. Any future bounty will be announced on our official website and channels.

## Official channels

Communication from the Tavorix team comes only from addresses at **@tavorix.xyz**.
We will never ask for your private keys or seed phrase.
